FAQ's

How does the Vendor Price Comparison feature work?

Simply upload your vendor price Excel file, select the suppliers you want to compare, and Mediastra instantly generates a side-by-side price comparison. You'll immediately see the lowest price, price differences, percentage differences, and potential savings for every item.

What type of file do I need to upload?

You can upload a simple Excel sheet (.xls or .xlsx) with the following columns: Item Name, Quantity, Unit Price/Rate, MRP, and Supplier Name. Optional fields like Pack or SCM are also supported.

Do I need any technical knowledge to use this?

Not at all. The system is built for simplicity. Just upload your file and Mediastra handles the comparison automatically — no formulas, no manual work, no complex setup.

How accurate is the price comparison?

Extremely accurate. Mediastra analyzes every price entry, highlights the best vendor, and clearly shows where you're overpaying. Even small differences (₹1–₹2) are detected so you never miss hidden savings.

Will this help me negotiate better rates with suppliers?

Yes. Since Mediastra shows competitor pricing and best market prices, you can negotiate confidently. Pharmacies often use these reports to secure better deals or match competitor rates.

What if my vendors have different pack sizes or pricing formats?

The system automatically reads different formats and standardizes them for comparison. If pack mismatches or unusual variations exist, the system will highlight them clearly.
